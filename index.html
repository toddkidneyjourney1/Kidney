THINKING ABOUT DONATING?

Start with questions, not pressure.

What is living kidney donation?

A living donor gives one healthy kidney to a recipient. Potential donors go through a medical and psychosocial evaluation to determine whether donation is appropriate for them.

Do I have to decide right away?

No. Learning more does not commit you to becoming a donor. Use the transplant center's official donor resources for the most accurate information.

What if I can't donate?

Sharing Todd's story is incredibly useful. The right donor may be someone you know, or someone they know.
